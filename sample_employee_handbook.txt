
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - VACATION POLICY

Vacation Requests:
Employees must submit vacation requests at least 30 days before their planned leave. Requests should be submitted to their direct manager for approval.

Vacation Balance:
Full-time employees receive 15 vacation days per calendar year. Unused vacation days are handled according to the company's applicable carryover policy.

Approval Process:
Managers review vacation requests based on staffing requirements and business needs. Approval must be received before employees make non-refundable travel arrangements.

Emergency Leave:
Employees with unexpected emergencies should contact their direct manager as soon as reasonably possible.
