
COMPANY IT SUPPORT POLICY

Technical Support:
Employees experiencing technical problems with company equipment should contact the IT support team at helpdesk@example.com.

The IT support team aims to respond to support requests within one business day.

Company Equipment:
Employees are responsible for maintaining company-issued laptops and equipment.

Employees must not share their company account passwords with other individuals.

EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Submitting Expenses:
Employees must submit expense reimbursement requests within 30 calendar days of the original purchase.

Employees must include an itemized receipt with every reimbursement request.

Approval Process:
All reimbursement requests must be approved by the employee's direct manager.

Payment:
Approved reimbursements are generally processed during the next regular payroll cycle.
